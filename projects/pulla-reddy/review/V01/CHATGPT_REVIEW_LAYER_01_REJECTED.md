# Independent Review | Layer 01 | REJECTED

## Decision
REJECT.

The previous provisional approval is revoked.

## Why
The production loop allowed Layer 01 to advance based too heavily on Codex-authored metadata and self-QA rather than an independent visual design review. That is not acceptable for this project. Technical continuity checks are necessary but they do not prove that the lighting is visually convincing, appetising, restrained, spatially coherent, or presentation-worthy.

The design owner has also explicitly rejected the current View 01 layers. That rejection overrides prior provisional approval.

## Required reset
- Layer 00 remains the only approved visual base for V01.
- Layer 01 is no longer approved.
- Do not proceed to Layer 02.
- Rework Layer 01 from approved Layer 00.

## Rework intent
Layer 01 must read as a deliberate architectural-lighting intervention, not as generic brightening.

The front sweet display counters are the hero. The new downlights must:
- feel like deep recessed architectural downlights, never glowing ceiling dots;
- create controlled, believable pools with visible direction and falloff;
- make the sweets richer and more appetising without flattening the counter;
- preserve darkness elsewhere so hierarchy is obvious;
- avoid broad ambient contamination;
- avoid glass glare, floating white highlights, or unrealistic luminous patches;
- remain fully 3000K;
- preserve the exact camera, architecture, ceiling grid, counters, products and Layer 00 darkness outside physically necessary spill.

## Independent-review requirement
Do not treat numerical continuity tests or SELF_QA PASS as design approval. Publish a new candidate and comparison package for independent review. The next step remains Layer 01 until an external reviewer explicitly approves it.

## Acceptance criteria
1. At first glance, the eye is pulled to the sweets, not the ceiling.
2. The recessed sources are visually quiet and subordinate to their light effect.
3. Counter illumination has believable direction, contrast and falloff.
4. Product colour feels warm and appetising, not orange or overexposed.
5. Glass remains clean and readable.
6. Unrelated walls, shelves, central displays and rear seating remain essentially Layer 00.
7. No exposure cheat or architecture drift.
8. The result should look like a professional lighting-design layer worthy of the Art Villa / Purva Bluebelle presentation language.
