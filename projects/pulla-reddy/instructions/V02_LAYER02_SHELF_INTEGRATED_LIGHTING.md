## Required action

Create V02 Layer 02 by adding only concealed integrated 3000K shelf lighting to the visible wall-mounted shelf compositions.

The integrated light must supplement the approved recessed wall-wash, not replace it.

The intended character is the quiet display-shelf language locked in the project rules: the source disappears and the shelf/product surfaces gain a gentle warm presence. The result must not look like generic cabinet strip lighting.

## Lighting intent

- Keep the approved Layer 01 wall-wash exactly frozen in intensity, distribution, colour and beam character.
- Add concealed integrated 3000K light only where the visible wall-mounted shelf geometry can physically hide the source.
- The shelf/product surfaces should become more legible and tactile without turning into luminous bands.
- Light should softly graze or illuminate the displayed shelf zone, with believable attenuation across the shelf depth and nearby wall.
- Preserve hierarchy: shelving becomes richer and more intentional, while the hospitality seating and planter remain deliberately calmer for later dedicated lighting layers.
- The source must remain invisible from the locked camera.

## Rejectable visual failures

Do not publish if any of the following are present:

- visible LED strip line;
- visible individual LED dots;
- a continuous neon-like edge;
- shelf fronts glowing as luminous objects;
- perfectly uniform flat brightness across every shelf;
- hard digital masks or airbrushed glow patches;
- integrated light overpowering the approved wall-wash;
- unrelated ceiling, floor, seating, planter or circulation activation;
- orange or amber colour cast inconsistent with believable 3000K white light;
- new visible fixtures, pendants, tracks or surface luminaires.
