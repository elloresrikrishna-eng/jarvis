## Design target

The cumulative hierarchy must remain:

1. principal sweet counters strongest;
2. wall merchandise and shelf compositions second;
3. central freestanding merchandise third;
4. rear seating newly readable but clearly softer and calmer than all retail layers;
5. ceiling visually quiet and subordinate.

The seating layer must be clear enough to communicate as a distinct presentation layer, but it must never make the rear zone as punchy as the sweet counters or shelves.

## Lighting character

- 3000K only.
- Deep recessed / visually quiet ceiling sources only.
- No pendants, tracks, surface spots or decorative luminaires.
- Use small, believable comfort pools over tables / seating where physically appropriate.
- Keep beam edges soft enough for hospitality use but not so diffuse that they become a broad ambient wash.
- Avoid glowing ceiling apertures, perfect airbrushed circles or floating light with no source logic.
- Preserve realistic shadowing from tables, chairs and seating.
- Do not brighten the rear wall generally unless physically caused by the released seating circuit.
- Keep seating materials warm and readable without orange cast or flattened contrast.

Reject and iterate locally before publishing if the first attempt:

- makes the rear seating compete with the sweet counters, shelves or central merchandise;
- creates broad ambient lift across the back half of the room;
- introduces visible fixture dots or decorative sources;
- changes the approved central floor pool or any retail-lighting family;
- changes unrelated glass, counter or shelf reflections;
- creates painted-on, perfectly circular or source-incoherent light pools;
- is so weak that the seating layer is not presentation-legible;
- produces mixed-CCT contamination or excessive orange cast.
