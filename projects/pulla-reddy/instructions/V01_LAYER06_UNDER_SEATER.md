## Design target

The cumulative hierarchy must remain:

1. principal sweet counters strongest;
2. wall merchandise and shelf compositions second;
3. central freestanding merchandise third;
4. rear seating comfort fourth;
5. under-seater contribution subtle and subordinate within the rear hospitality zone;
6. ceiling visually quiet and subordinate.

The under-seater layer must be presentation-legible when compared directly with Layer 05, but it should read primarily as a soft reflected floor glow rather than as a luminous line source.

## Lighting character

- 3000K only.
- Source fully concealed beneath the built-in seater.
- No visible LED strip line, diode texture or luminous ribbon.
- Light should graze the immediate floor softly and fade quickly.
- Keep the apparent contribution low enough that the banquette does not look like it is floating dramatically.
- Preserve realistic contact shadows at legs, furniture edges and adjacent objects.
- Avoid an even continuous neon-like band.
- Avoid broad floor brightening beyond the physically plausible under-seater spill zone.
- Do not brighten the rear wall, tables or chairs except for physically unavoidable local bounce.
- Do not change the Layer 05 recessed seating-comfort pools.
