## Visual intent

The shelf-integrated contribution should make the merchandise feel gently self-illuminated while the light source remains invisible.

Target character:

- restrained warm 3000K product/shelf glow;
- hidden source with no visible LED strip line, LED dots or bright shelf-edge ribbon;
- soft local illumination on products and the immediate shelf plane;
- wall-wash remains legible as a separate ceiling-origin lighting family;
- brass shelf supports may receive natural material highlights but must not read as luminous rods or sources;
- merchandise remains the second visual tier behind the sweet counters;
- no hospitality, central-display or circulation lighting is introduced;
- no global exposure or gamma lift.
